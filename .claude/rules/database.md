# Database rules

## Entities

Entities in `entities/` are generated by `sea-orm-cli`.
Do not edit them manually.
Re-run the generator when the schema changes.

Exception: annotations marked `// KEEP ME` (and any associated `// SYNC` markers) are intentional manual additions.
They are not produced by the generator and must be re-applied by hand after regenerating.

## Migrations

Write migrations using `SchemaManager` DDL methods, not raw SQL.
